Privacy notice

Mini Nightly Backup is a private utility for its account owner. The utility is not a public service and does not collect registrations from other users.

Google access and processing

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission: access to the specific files used with this application. It creates encrypted archives and verification receipts in a dedicated private folder, reads them back to verify integrity, and reads ownership and permission metadata to check private access.

Backup content is encrypted before upload. Google Drive stores encrypted archives and operational receipts. OAuth credentials and the normal decryption identity are retained in private local configuration, outside the backup source. Small restored samples and status files are retained in a private local folder for verification, within a fixed operating-data limit.

If the owner separately creates a password-encrypted recovery-key copy, Drive receives that encrypted wrapper. Its wrapping password and plaintext identity are not uploaded.

Use, sharing and retention

The utility does not sell data, serve advertising, call AI services, or use Drive files to train models. Archive contents are not published. The owner may separately use authorized administration tools to inspect operational metadata and verification results.

Cloud recovery points are retained until the owner removes them; automatic cloud pruning is not currently enabled. Failed candidates may remain for inspection and are not verified recovery points. Local status files and verification samples are replaced as the utility runs. Existing protected recovery archives are managed separately.

Owner control

The account owner can revoke this application’s access through Google Account connection controls and can remove its files through Google Drive. Revocation does not itself delete stored archives. Questions about this privately operated utility should be directed to its account owner.
