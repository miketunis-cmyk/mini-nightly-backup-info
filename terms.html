Use and recovery conditions

Mini Nightly Backup is intended only for private operation by its account owner. It is not offered to other users as a public backup service.

The utility captures file payloads and consistent individual SQLite database snapshots. It does not provide an atomic whole-application, browser LevelDB, or whole-machine image. Recovery limitations and any files that changed during capture are recorded in verification evidence.

A failed or interrupted run is not a verified recovery point. Verification requires complete authenticated readback, manifest checks and actual representative file/database restores. A receipt reports the checks performed; it is not a guarantee that every application will restart correctly.

Recovery requires access to the encrypted archive and the matching decryption identity. A password-encrypted recovery-key wrapper also requires its separately retained password. The owner must retain the required recovery material independently of the machine being protected.

The operator must review capacity, retention and actual restore evidence before replacing source data. This utility does not automatically overwrite or restart the source applications.
